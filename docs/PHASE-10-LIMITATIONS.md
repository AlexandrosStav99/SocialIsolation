# Phase 10 - Limitations and Production Requirements

- University MVP / controlled demonstration, not a production social, healthcare or emergency system.
- Safety trigger matrix, wording and immediate-support resources still require qualified domain-expert validation.
- Target-user usability validation with adults 18–30 remains external human research.
- WCAG 2.2 AA is a target; no compliance certification is claimed.
- Real deployment requires Cyprus/EU legal and privacy review, operational retention decisions and provider agreements.
- Payload CMS runtime integration remains future implementation work; current foundations/contracts do not equal a deployed Payload-backed directory.
- Production authentication/IdP, MFA, infrastructure hardening, monitoring, incident response and security assessment remain outside this university implementation.
- Checked service information ages and needs an operational re-check process before real use.
- AI remains assistive and must never become the routing, safety, eligibility or handoff decision-maker.
